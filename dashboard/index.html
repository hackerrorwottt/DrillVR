Launch Mobile VR Training Drill

To initiate a live emergency drill on an Android smartphone:

  1. Ensure the trainee's phone has DrillVR.apk installed.
  2. Connect the phone to the same local Wi-Fi as your development machine.
  3. Set the Unity ApiClient Base URL to your laptop's LAN IP: http://<YOUR_IP>:4000.
  4. Insert the phone into a Google Cardboard viewer and select Login & Sync.
